Our story

Support local artisans. Celebrate handmade.

Thread & Glow GB celebrates handmade creativity from Gilgit-Baltistan: warm Himalayan salt lamps and crochet pieces stitched thread by thread. Every item is made by hand, so each one has its own character.

Questions

How do I place and pay for an order?

Add items to your cart and send your order on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm availability, final prices, delivery options and the payment method with you before your order is finalized. No payment is taken on this website.

Do you deliver?

Message us with your city or delivery address and we’ll confirm whether delivery is available, along with the charges and estimated timing.

Are salt lamps fragile?

Salt can chip, so lamps are wrapped carefully for delivery.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Return and exchange options depend on the item. Please confirm the applicable terms with us on WhatsApp before placing your order.

Can I request a custom colour or gift set?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp with your idea.
